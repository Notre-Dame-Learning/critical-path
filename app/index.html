Your first project

  • Each turn is one week. Spend Action Points on moves, then end the week.
  • Situations come up at least every other week. Several answers are partly right; one is best. Escalating to your supervisor PM is always allowed, and the debrief shows what you could have done yourself.
  • Mistakes hide. Rework you don't find early shows up late.
  • The course opens on launch day whether it's ready or not.
