  • Each turn is one week. You meet your faculty partner every week: pick that meeting's focus (free), spend Action Points on team moves, then end the week.
  • The schedule bar shows whether you're ahead, on track or behind the pace needed to launch on time. When you're behind, asking someone for extra hours helps, at a cost to morale and quality.
  • A situation comes up at least every other week. Escalating to Dwayneah is always allowed, and the debrief shows what you could have done yourself.
  • Mistakes hide. Rework you don't find early shows up late. The course opens on launch day whether it's ready or not.
